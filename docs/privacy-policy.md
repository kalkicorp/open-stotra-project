# Privacy Policy
## Manas: Aarti & Bhakti Sagar

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Developer:** Kalki Corporation

---

### 1. Overview
**Manas: Aarti & Bhakti Sagar** is built with user privacy and peace of mind as a core priority. This application does not require user accounts, does not track user behavior, and does not collect personally identifiable information.

---

### 2. Information Collection and Use
* **Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.
* **Local Storage:** Your reading preferences—such as font size adjustments, theme mode (Warm Paper or Dark Mode), and inline **Sthān** bookmarks—are stored strictly locally on your device. This data never leaves your phone.

---

### 3. Network Requests & Text Updates
The app connects to the internet solely to check for and download updated devotional text files from our public content repository (**Open Stotra Project** hosted on GitHub).

* During this sync process, standard network metadata (such as your IP address) is processed by GitHub's servers according to their standard infrastructure policies.
* **Kalki Corporation** does not log, collect, or store any of this network data.

---

### 4. Advertisements and Third-Party Analytics
**Manas: Aarti & Bhakti Sagar** is completely ad-free and does not integrate third-party tracking SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or advertising networks.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any personal data, our application is safe for users of all ages and complies with children's privacy standards.

---

### 6. Changes to This Policy
If we introduce new features in the future (such as optional cloud backups or non-intrusive support options), we will update this Privacy Policy before releasing those changes.

---
