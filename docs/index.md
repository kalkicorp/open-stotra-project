## Why Manas?
Designed like a mindful digital book rather than a cluttered utility, **Manas** brings peace back to your daily prayers with crisp Devanagari typography and zero interruptions.

* **Ad-Free Sanctuary:** No flashing banners or disruptive pop-ups. Just a warm, paper-toned interface built for morning and evening rituals.
* **Sthān (स्थान) Inline Bookmarks:** Double-tap any individual verse or shloka to save your exact reading spot and resume long texts effortlessly.
* **Gods-First Library:** Neatly organized by deity and category. Browse Aartis, Chalisas, Stotrams, and Bhajans offline anytime.
* **Customizable Reading:** Adjust Devanagari text sizes easily and switch between warm paper mode and dark mode.
* **Private by design:** No tracking, no analytics SDKs, and reading preferences stay on your device. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-policy.html' | relative_url }}).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Manas free?**
Yes. Manas is free to download and has no advertisements.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. Texts are available offline. The app only goes online to fetch updated text files.

**Which texts are included?**
Aartis, Chalisas, Stotrams and Bhajans, organized by deity, in Devanagari script.

**Which devices are supported?**
Android phones and tablets, via [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kalkicorp.manas).
